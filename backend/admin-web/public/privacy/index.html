3. Location data — important details

TRAPP accesses your location in the background so that traps can trigger automatically even when your screen is off or you have switched to another app. This is the core mechanic of the game.

Your location is never shared with other players. Only the coordinates of traps you have placed are stored and visible within the game — not your current position.

You can revoke background location permission at any time in your device Settings → Apps → Trapp → Permissions. The game will continue to work but traps will only trigger while the app is open.

4. Third-party services we use

  • Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting, FCM) — data stored in EU/EEA regions where available. Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdMob — serves ads to free-tier users. May use the Advertising ID for personalisation. Google Privacy Policy
  • Revolut Ltd — payment processing for subscriptions and donations. We pass your purchase intent to Revolut's hosted checkout; we never see or store your card details. Revolut Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps SDK — renders the in-game map. Subject to Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Data retention

  • Account data: retained until you delete your account
  • Location data (trap coordinates): deleted when the trap is disarmed or expires
  • Game stats and scores: retained for the lifetime of your account
  • Support tickets: retained for 3 years
  • Payment records: retained for 8 years (Hungarian accounting law requirement)

6. Your rights under GDPR

As a data subject in the EU/EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Restriction — ask us to pause processing of your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — for processing based on consent (e.g. AdMob personalisation, contacts access) at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@playtrapnow.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to delete your account directly from within the app (Settings → Delete Account), which triggers immediate erasure of your personal data.

8. Children

TRAPP is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 16 without verified parental consent. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account without consent, contact us at privacy@playtrapnow.com and we will delete the account promptly.

9. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Firebase Firestore data is encrypted at rest. We apply the principle of least privilege — only the minimum necessary data is stored and processed for each feature.
