Account & data deletion

Delete your TrApp account

This page explains how to delete your account in TrApp (listed on Google Play as “Trap”, developed by Fairco), which data is deleted, which records we must keep in anonymised form, and how to ask for deletion if you no longer have the app.

01 Delete your account in the app

  1. Open TrApp and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap “Delete account”.
  4. Read the summary and confirm the deletion.

Your account and data are erased immediately and you are signed out. This cannot be undone: your username, progress, traps, rankings and purchases tied to the account cannot be restored.

If you have an active subscription, it is cancelled at the moment of deletion and will not renew. Subscriptions bought through Google Play are managed by Google: please also cancel them in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

02 What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted permanently

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (e-mail address, password)
  • Profile: username, display name, profile picture, birth date, settings, language
  • Your traps, trap inventory, items and awards
  • Your location data and last known position
  • Scores, statistics and leaderboard entries
  • Friends, friend requests, invitations and ally group memberships
  • Chat conversations and messages
  • Support tickets and their attachments
  • Notifications and push notification tokens

Kept in anonymised form

  • Payment, donation, subscription and invoice records — kept for 8 years, as required by Hungarian accounting law. Your name, e-mail address and user ID are removed; only the amount, date, currency and payment provider reference remain.
  • Game events shared with other players (e.g. a capture between you and another player) — kept without your user ID, username or location so the other player’s history stays consistent.
  • A security log entry that an account was deleted (date and internal ID only, no name or e-mail).

04 Request deletion without the app

If you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, send us an e-mail from the address registered to your TrApp account. Please include your username if you remember it.

E-mail privacy@playtrapnow.com

We verify that the request comes from the account owner, delete the account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail. If we cannot verify ownership from the registered address, we may ask for additional confirmation.
